Safari, and every browser on iPhone and iPad, erase a site’s stored data after seven days without a visit. Your Medications, dose records and Notes go with it. There is no copy on a server to restore from, so pillage is not recommended in this browser.

If you stay: add pillage to your Home Screen, which exempts it from that rule, and export a backup regularly from Data.
